## Evidence

- Shift 102 localized the pinned writer defect: MetaCryptoKeyOSVersion is typed uint32 at APSB offset 108 but the pinned writer emits zero.
- Shift 103 reduced the repair to an executable bounded same-block path: resolve rebuilt volume paddr, patch only offset 108, reseal Fletcher64 over block[8:], validate checksum, write the same block.
- Shift 104 made no target mutation because the connector returned a truncated `main.go` while its available write primitive required complete replacement.
- The engineering discriminator and repair boundary are therefore already sufficient; the immediate blocker is lossless acquisition of the authoritative target preimage, not APFS semantics.

## Decision

Do not reopen APFS architecture. Replace DIR-026 with DIR-027, which makes lossless preimage acquisition an explicit mandatory first step and permits deterministic reconstruction from bounded non-overlapping GitHub reads followed by verification against the authoritative blob SHA before any whole-file CAS replacement.

Production remains enabled. No owner decision, STOP, or transfer is required.
