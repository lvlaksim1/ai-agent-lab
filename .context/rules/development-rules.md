# Development Rules

- Evidence-driven smallest justified change.
- SHA/CAS-aware writes for mutable control-plane state.
- Re-read/merge on conflicts.
- Preserve tests/proof gates/Definition of Done.
- Unverified claims do not count as progress.
- Technical evidence lives in journals/reviews; human reports stay readable.
- Queue events are object-scoped.
- Transfers drain/accept work safely.
- Brigade rating is global and rotation stays fair.
- Published reports are immutable; `latest.md` is not a publication trigger.
- Context history is append-preserving; decisions are superseded, not silently rewritten.
